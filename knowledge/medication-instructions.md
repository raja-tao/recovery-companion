# Taking Your Medicines After Surgery

SYNTHETIC SAMPLE CONTENT FOR A PROOF OF CONCEPT. NOT FOR CLINICAL USE.

## Taking medicines as prescribed
Take your medicines exactly as written on your medication list. Do not change how much you take or how often unless your prescriber tells you to. If you have questions about a dose, contact your pharmacist or care team.

## Opioid pain medicine safety
Opioid pain medicine can make you sleepy and constipated. Do not drive, drink alcohol, or take sleeping pills while taking it. Store it in a locked place away from children and others.

## Blood thinners
Your blood thinner helps prevent blood clots after surgery. You may bruise more easily. Use a soft toothbrush and an electric razor. Tell your care team about any unusual bleeding.

## Stool softeners
A stool softener helps prevent constipation caused by pain medicine. Drink plenty of water while taking it.

## Disposing of unused medicine
Bring unused opioid medicine to a pharmacy take-back location. Do not share your medicine with anyone.
