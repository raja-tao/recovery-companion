# After Your Surgery — Frequently Asked Questions

SYNTHETIC SAMPLE CONTENT FOR A PROOF OF CONCEPT. NOT FOR CLINICAL USE.

## Hydration
Drink 6 to 8 glasses of water or other non-alcoholic fluids each day unless your doctor has limited your fluids. Good hydration helps prevent constipation and dizziness.

## Constipation
Pain medicine and less activity often cause constipation. Drink plenty of fluids, eat fruits, vegetables and whole grains, and walk regularly. Take the stool softener on your medication list as prescribed. Tell your care team if you have not had a bowel movement in 3 days.

## Alcohol
Do not drink alcohol while you are taking opioid pain medicine or a blood thinner. Ask your care team before drinking alcohol during your recovery.

## Sleep
It is common to have trouble sleeping for a few weeks after surgery. Keep a regular bedtime, limit naps to 30 minutes, and take your pain medicine as prescribed before bed if you need it.

## Travel
Avoid long car rides and flights for the first 4 weeks. If you must travel, stop or stand up every hour to walk and do ankle pumps. Tell your care team about any travel plans.

## The daily check-in
Complete your 2-minute check-in every day, ideally in the morning. Your answers are checked against your surgeon's warning signs, and your care team is alerted if something needs attention.

## Contacting your care team
For non-urgent questions, send a message in the app or call the nurse line during business hours. A nurse will respond within one business day. For urgent problems, call the nurse line any time.
