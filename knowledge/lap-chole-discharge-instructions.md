# Laparoscopic Gallbladder Removal — Surgeon Discharge Instructions

SYNTHETIC SAMPLE CONTENT FOR A PROOF OF CONCEPT. NOT FOR CLINICAL USE.

## Diet
Start with light, low-fat meals for the first few days. Greasy or fried foods may cause bloating or diarrhea. Add your usual foods back slowly as you feel ready.

## Driving
Do not drive for at least 1 week, and never while taking opioid pain medicine. You should be able to make sudden movements, such as braking, without pain before you drive.

## Showering and bathing
You may shower 24 hours after surgery. Let soapy water run over the small incisions and pat them dry. Do not soak in a bath or swim for 2 weeks.

## Wound care
The small incisions are closed with skin glue or adhesive strips. Leave them in place; they will fall off on their own in 7 to 10 days. Report increasing redness, drainage, or opening.

## Shoulder pain
Pain in your shoulder for 1 to 2 days is common. It is caused by the gas used during surgery. Walking helps it go away.

## Walking and activity
Walk several times a day starting the day of surgery. Do not lift anything heavier than 10 pounds for 2 weeks.

## Lifting
Do not lift anything heavier than 10 pounds, such as a full laundry basket or a child, for 2 weeks after surgery.

## Return to work
Most people return to desk work in 1 to 2 weeks. Jobs with heavy lifting may need 4 weeks.

## When to contact your care team
Contact your care team the same day for a temperature of 101°F or higher, yellowing of the skin or eyes, worsening belly pain, or vomiting that does not stop.

## Call 911
Call 911 for chest pain, trouble breathing, or fainting.
