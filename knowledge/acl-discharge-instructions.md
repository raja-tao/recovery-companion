# ACL Reconstruction — Surgeon Discharge Instructions

SYNTHETIC SAMPLE CONTENT FOR A PROOF OF CONCEPT. NOT FOR CLINICAL USE.

## Brace and crutches
Wear your knee brace locked straight when walking and sleeping until your surgeon or physical therapist tells you to unlock it. Use crutches and put weight on your leg as instructed.

## Driving
If your right knee was operated on, do not drive until you are off crutches and out of the locked brace, usually 4 to 6 weeks. Never drive while taking opioid pain medicine.

## Showering and bathing
You may shower 72 hours after surgery. Cover the dressing with a plastic bag and sit on a shower chair. Do not soak your knee for 4 weeks.

## Swelling and ice
Use your cold therapy unit or ice packs for 20 minutes every 2 hours while awake for the first week. Elevate your leg above your heart.

## Physical therapy (PT)
Physical therapy starts 3 to 7 days after surgery. Early PT focuses on getting your knee fully straight and reducing swelling.

## Home exercises
Do quad sets, straight-leg raises in the brace, ankle pumps, and heel props 3 times a day.

## Sleeping position
Sleep on your back with the brace locked straight and your heel propped on a pillow. Do not put a pillow under your knee.

## When to contact your care team
Contact your care team the same day for a temperature of 101°F or higher, increasing redness or drainage from the incision, or calf pain.

## Call 911
Call 911 for chest pain or trouble breathing.
